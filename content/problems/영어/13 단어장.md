# 13 단어장

## ache (v.)
뜻: to have or suffer a dull but constant pain
예문: My legs ached after I ran a marathon.
유의어: hurt

## concentrate (v.)
뜻: to think about and focus your mind on something
예문: You really need to focus and concentrate on the problem if you want to find the best solution.
유의어: focus

## discovery (n.)
뜻: something found for the first time
예문: Scientists are excited about the new discovery of a planet that is a lot like Earth.
유의어: find, finding

## educate (v.)
뜻: to provide knowledge, training, and guidance to
예문: The responsibility of a school is to educate children.
유의어: coach, instruct, teach

## effort (n.)
뜻: the use of energy to complete a task or do a job; hard work
예문: It took a lot of effort to hike up the mountain.
유의어: energy, work

## improve (v.)
뜻: to make something better
예문: I studied hard to improve my grades.
유의어: better
반의어: worsen

## inspire (v.)
뜻: to fill someone with ideas, courage, or enthusiasm
예문: My father inspires me to be a kind person.
유의어: encourage
반의어: discourage

## satisfy (v.)
뜻: to meet someone's needs or wishes
예문: The cold glass of milk satisfied his thirst.
유의어: delight, please

## scamper (v.)
뜻: to run or move quickly or playfully
예문: A squirrel quickly scampered across the lawn.
유의어: dash, race, scurry

## sore (adj.)
뜻: full of or causing pain
예문: His muscles were sore after the long hike.
유의어: aching, hurting, painful
반의어: painless

## growl (v.)
뜻: to make a noise that shows anger, dissatisfaction, or pain
예문: My younger brother growled when he saw that I was sitting in his favorite chair.
유의어: grumble, moan

## tangled (adj.)
뜻: twisted or knotted together, especially in a complicated or confusing way
예문: Tim's foot is stuck in the tangled rope.
유의어: knotted, twisted
