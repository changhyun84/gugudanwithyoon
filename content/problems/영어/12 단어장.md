# 12 단어장

## clever (adj.)
뜻: having a quick mind; smart
예문: My pet dog is clever and can do many tricks.
유의어: smart
반의어: dumb

## contain (v.)
뜻: to hold or keep inside
예문: The old box contained jewels, coins, and other treasures.
유의어: hold

## gasp (v.)
뜻: to breathe in suddenly because of surprise, shock, or pain
예문: I gasped for air because I swallowed some water.

## grateful (adj.)
뜻: feeling thankful
예문: I am grateful for my loving family.
유의어: thankful, appreciative
반의어: ungrateful, unappreciative

## humble (adj.)
뜻: not special, fancy, or expensive
예문: Our humble home isn't big, but we love it.
유의어: modest
반의어: grand

## identical (adj.)
뜻: being the same in every way
예문: It is hard to tell apart the identical twins.
반의어: different

## lean (v.)
뜻: to bend down or over
예문: We leaned over the edge to see the view below.

## odd (adj.)
뜻: strange or unusual
예문: Do you think it is odd to look for buried treasure?
유의어: strange, weird, unusual
반의어: normal, usual

## plenty (n.)
뜻: an amount that is enough
예문: There was plenty of cake for everyone in the class to eat.

## startled (adj.)
뜻: shocked or surprised by something sudden
예문: The diver was startled to find treasure at the bottom of the ocean.
유의어: shocked, surprised

## toss (v.)
뜻: to throw something lightly
예문: We tossed our hats into the air.
유의어: fling, chuck

## village (n.)
뜻: a small town
예문: We live in a small village that is near the ocean.
유의어: town
