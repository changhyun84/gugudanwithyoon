# 14 단어장

## celebrate (v.)
뜻: to do something special to praise or honor an important act, event, occasion, etc.
예문: Let's go out for ice cream to celebrate after your play.

## courage (n.)
뜻: the ability or willingness to do something that is dangerous, difficult, painful, etc.
예문: John's little brother showed a lot of courage by standing up to the bully.
유의어: boldness, bravery
반의어: cowardice, cowardliness

## disappointment (n.)
뜻: a feeling of unhappiness or something that causes unhappiness because something is not as good as you expected or did not happen the way that you hoped
예문: I felt disappointment when my favorite baseball team lost.
유의어: sadness, sorrow
반의어: satisfaction

## precious (adj.)
뜻: greatly loved, adored, or important
예문: My watch is precious to me because my grandfather gave it to me.
유의어: cherished, dear, special

## pride (n.)
뜻: a feeling of happiness you get when you or someone you know does something well
예문: Alex is a hard worker, and he takes pride in his work.
유의어: joy, pleasure
반의어: shame

## remind (v.)
뜻: to make someone remember; to cause to remember
예문: My mother reminded me to wash my hands before dinner.

## symbol (n.)
뜻: something, such as a picture or an object, that stands for something else
예문: Doves are a symbol of peace and friendship.
유의어: sign

## tradition (n.)
뜻: a belief, idea, or practice that has been passed down over time
예문: My family has a tradition of decorating a Christmas tree each year.
유의어: custom, practice, ritual

## culture (n.)
뜻: the language, customs, ideas, and art of a particular group of people
예문: I studied Japan's history and art to learn more about Japanese culture.

## presentation (n.)
뜻: a lecture or speech given in front of an audience
예문: I had to give a 10-minute presentation in front of my class today.
유의어: lecture, speech, talk

## belief (n.)
뜻: something that a person accepts as acceptable, right, or true
예문: Jeff has the belief that aliens exist.
유의어: opinion
반의어: disbelief, doubt

## strength (n.)
뜻: the quality or state of being strong
예문: Bill is doing a lot of exercise to build up his physical strength.
유의어: might, power
반의어: weakness
